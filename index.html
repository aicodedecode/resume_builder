Why resumes get rejected

Software reads your resume before a human does.

Applicant-tracking systems filter out resumes they can’t parse — and graphics, tables, text boxes, and fancy layouts get mangled or tossed. Recruiters spend about 6 seconds on the resumes that survive.

This builder flips the process: it scores your resume the way a parser would — before you submit — and tells you exactly what to fix.
